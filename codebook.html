aw_data_civcas_incidents
Central incident records · 70 fields
▼
The primary dataset containing unique records of reported civilian harm incidents. Each row represents a single incident with details on timing, location, military action, assessment grading, casualty counts, and source tracking. All other tables link back to this table via post_id and code.

Tracks the reported perpetrators (military forces, coalitions, militant groups) associated with each incident, including their public statements, assessments, conceded casualties, and geolocation data.

Damage & destruction records · 8 fields
▼
Documents infrastructure damaged or destroyed in each incident, categorised by facility type (e.g. hospital, school, residential) with information on whether the target was declared and the extent of destruction.

Individual victim records · 18 fields
▼
Individual-level records for named victims, including personal details (name, profession, gender, age), whether they were killed or injured, and family group tracking for reconciliation purposes.
